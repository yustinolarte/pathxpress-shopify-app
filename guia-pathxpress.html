Section 01

What is PATHXPRESS?

PATHXPRESS Portal is an app embedded in the Shopify admin that connects your store to the PathXpress courier network to automate the entire shipping cycle.

From the app you can send orders to the courier, generate waybill numbers, print labels, track each shipment, handle returns and exchanges, and reconcile the money collected on delivery (COD). All without leaving your Shopify admin.

What it does for you

  • Syncs orders from Shopify to PathXpress — automatically, by tag, or manually.
  • Generates waybills & labels ready to print from each shipment.
  • Calculates shipping rates at checkout (dynamic or fixed rates).
  • Tracks the status of every package with a full history.
  • Reconciles COD: shows what is pending collection, collected and already remitted to you.
  • Processes returns and exchanges approved in Shopify automatically.
  • Rescues orders that failed to sync with one click.

Section 02

Prerequisites

Before installing the app, make sure you have the following:

  • An active Shopify store with admin permissions.
  • Your PathXpress Client ID (provided by the PathXpress team; you'll need it during setup).
  • For dynamic rates at checkout: a Shopify Advanced/Plus plan or the “third-party calculated shipping rates” add-on. On the Basic plan, the app offers fixed rates as an alternative.
✓
Don't know your Client ID? Contact PathXpress support. Without a valid Client ID the app cannot create shipments.

Permissions the app requests

During installation, Shopify will ask you to authorize these scopes so the app can read orders and create shipments and returns:

PermissionReason
read_orders / write_ordersRead and update orders.
read_fulfillments / write_fulfillmentsCreate and update fulfillments.
read_shipping / write_shippingCalculate and register shipping rates.
read_returns / write_returnsProcess returns and exchanges.

Install & connect

The first time you open the app inside Shopify, it isn't connected to your PathXpress account yet. You'll see this welcome screen:

Section 03 · steps

Steps to connect

  1. Open the app in Shopify. Go to Apps → PATHXPRESS Portal in your store admin.
  2. Click “Connect now”. This starts Shopify's authorization (OAuth) flow.
  3. Authorize the permissions. Shopify shows the list of scopes the app needs. Review and click Install.
  4. Done. You return to the app and the badge changes to Connected (green dot). Now you can configure it.
i
Persistent connection Once connected, the app remembers the access even if you log out or the server restarts. You only repeat this step if you uninstall the app.
Section 04

Initial setup (Settings)

After connecting, go to the Settings tab. This is where you define how PathXpress integrates with your store. It's the most important step.

Section 04 · detail

Each option, explained

1 · PathXpress Connection — Client ID

Enter the Client ID assigned to you by PathXpress. It identifies your account in the portal and is required: without it the app cannot create shipments or read your services.

2 · Order Sync Mode

Defines when Shopify orders are sent to PathXpress. Pick one:

ModeBehaviorBest for
⚡ AutomaticEvery new order is sent to PathXpress automatically when it's created in Shopify.Stores that ship every order with PathXpress.
🏷️ By TagOnly orders that have a specific Shopify tag (e.g. send_pathxpress) are synced.Stores using several couriers.
✋ ManualOrders appear in the Orders tab and you choose which to send.Full control, order by order.
i
“By Tag” modeWhen you choose this mode an extra field appears to type the exact tag. Only orders with that tag sync automatically.

3 · Default Shipping Service

The courier service applied to all orders from this store (for example DOM next-day 1–2 days, SDD same day, BULLET 4 hours). The list shows only the services enabled for your PathXpress account.

4 · Free Shipping Thresholds

Minimum order amount to offer free shipping. There are two boxes: Standard (DOM) and Express (same day). Leave a box empty to disable free shipping for that service.

5 · Returns & Exchanges

If you tick the box, when you approve a return or exchange in Shopify it is automatically sent to PathXpress to schedule the pickup.

✓
Don't forget to saveClick Save Settings when finished. A green confirmation message appears at the top.
Section 05

Checkout shipping rates

So your customers see the PathXpress shipping cost at checkout, the app automatically registers a carrier service named PathXpress Shipping in your store.

If your Shopify plan does not have “third-party calculated rates” enabled, the Dashboard shows this notice with two options:

Option 1 — Dynamic rates (recommended)

In Shopify go to Settings → Shipping and delivery and enable “Third-party calculated rates”. This requires an Advanced/Plus plan or the matching add-on. With it, the checkout asks PathXpress for the real cost based on destination, weight and service.

Option 2 — Fixed rates (Basic plan)

On the Basic plan, click Create Fixed Rates. The app automatically creates a UAE shipping zone in Shopify with flat rates (Standard 15 AED, Express 25 AED). You can adjust them later from Shopify's shipping settings.

!
Don't see the notice?It means rates are already working. The notice only appears when rate calculation still needs to be enabled.
Section 06

The Dashboard

This is the home screen once the store is connected. It shows the day's shipping activity at a glance and the list of your recent shipments.

What you see on the Dashboard

  • Shipments today — shipments created today.
  • Active shipments — packages in transit across the network.
  • Pending COD (dark card) — money pending collection; click it to go to COD reconciliation.
  • My PathXpress shipments — shipments table with filters (All / Shipments / Returns / Exchanges) and a search box by order # or waybill.

Each row shows the order number, the waybill, the type, the status, the COD, the date, and actions: Print Label and Track. Orders not yet picked up also show a delete button.

Section 07

Order management (Orders)

Here you choose which Shopify orders to send to PathXpress. It's the key screen for Manual mode, though you can also use it to sync individual orders in any mode.

How to send orders to PathXpress

  1. Open the Orders tab. You'll see your store's orders. The blue strip shows the active sync mode (with a Change link to Settings).
  2. Select the orders. Tick the Pending orders, or use Select all pending. Already-synced orders show their waybill and have no checkbox.
  3. Click “Sync Selected (n)”. The button shows how many you'll send. Depending on your setup, it may ask you to pick the service applied to all of them.
  4. Check the result. Each row turns Synced with its waybill number in the Status column.
✓
Missing orders in the list?Use Find All Unsynced Orders so the app scans your whole store for orders not yet sent to PathXpress.
Section 08

Labels & waybills

Every synced shipment generates a waybill number and a label ready to print and stick on the package.

Printing a label

  1. Find the shipment. On the Dashboard (“My PathXpress shipments” table) search by order number or waybill.
  2. Click “Print Label”. The app opens the official waybill PDF generated by the PathXpress portal in a new tab.
  3. Print and attach. The PDF includes the barcode / QR with the shipment data for route scanning.
i
Official labelThe label comes directly from the PathXpress portal (single source of truth). If the portal is momentarily unavailable, the app generates an equivalent fallback label in the browser.
!
Is “Print Label” disabled?It means the shipment doesn't have a waybill yet (status “Pending”). Wait until the sync with PathXpress completes.
Section 09

Shipment tracking

From any shipment you can click Track to see its current status and the full history of movements.

The top section summarizes the current status, the service, the recipient, the destination, the COD (if any) and the creation date. Below, the timeline shows each movement: created, picked up, in transit, out for delivery and delivered.

Section 10

COD reconciliation

COD (Cash on Delivery) is the money the courier collects from your customer on delivery and then remits to you. This screen shows the status of that money.

The three states of the money

  • Pending collection — the package is out for delivery but the customer hasn't paid yet.
  • Collected (to remit) — already collected from the customer; awaiting PathXpress to remit it to you.
  • Remitted to you — the money is already settled in your account.

The table details each shipment with its amount, status and the collection and remittance dates. It's a read-only view for reconciliation; the courier's events keep it updated.

Section 11

Rescue stuck orders

Sometimes an order is created in PathXpress but isn't reflected correctly in Shopify (the fulfillment isn't marked, or the “delivered” event wasn't sent). The Rescue tab detects them and lets you retry.

How to use it

  • Without Shopify fulfillment — orders with a waybill in PathXpress but no fulfillment in Shopify.
  • Fulfilled but “Delivered” not sent — already fulfilled, but the delivery event is missing.
  • Force ALL — retries everything (fulfillment + delivered) at once.
  • Send “delivered” only — only resends the delivery event.
  • Retry (per row) — retries a single order.

The number next to Rescue in the navigation shows how many orders need attention. Ideally, keep it at zero.

Section 12

FAQ & troubleshooting

The app says “Not connected”. What do I do?

Click Connect now on the Dashboard and complete Shopify's authorization. If you'd already connected it, reopen the app from Apps in your admin.

I saved the Client ID but no shipments are created.

Check that the Client ID is correct and that your PathXpress account is active. Confirm with PathXpress support. Also review the sync mode in Settings.

My customers don't see the shipping cost at checkout.

You need to enable rates (Section 05). On Advanced/Plus enable third-party calculated rates; on Basic use Create Fixed Rates.

I chose “Automatic” mode but an order wasn't sent.

Go to Rescue and retry, or open Orders and use Find All Unsynced Orders to sync it manually.

Can I change the service or mode later?

Yes. Everything is adjustable in Settings at any time; remember to click Save Settings.

Do I collect the COD myself?

No. The courier collects it on delivery and then remits it to you. The COD screen is for reconciliation (pending, collected, remitted).

Recommended end-to-end flow

  1. Install & connect the app (Connect now → authorize in Shopify).
  2. Configure in Settings: Client ID, sync mode, default service, free shipping and returns. Save.
  3. Enable shipping rates (dynamic or fixed) if you charge for shipping at checkout.
  4. Operate daily: sync orders (auto/tag/manual), print labels and track.
  5. Reconcile COD and check Rescue regularly so nothing gets stuck.
